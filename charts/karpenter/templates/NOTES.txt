1. CRDs first, then the controller:

   helm upgrade --install karpenter-crd charts/karpenter-crd --namespace kube-system
   helm upgrade --install karpenter charts/karpenter --namespace kube-system \
     --set apiToken="<digitalocean-pat>" \
     --set settings.clusterName="<doks-cluster-name>"

2. Apply a DONodeClass and NodePool (see examples/ in the repo).

3. The controller reads DIGITALOCEAN_TOKEN from Secret {{ include "karpenter.credentialsSecretName" . }}.
